05 / OPERATING BOUNDARIES

Useful automation starts with clear limits.

ORELYN is a local acquisition and evidence tool, not a public redistribution service or a legal determination engine.

01

Authorized acquisition only

Requests are limited to public or authorized endpoints, with timeouts, bounded concurrency and no credential bypass.

02

Partial is not complete

A .part file records an incomplete transfer. Only a fully completed response is hashed and moved into the read-only source area.

03

Metadata is not legal advice

License labels are review aids. Always verify the provider’s current terms and attribution duties before reuse or publication.

04

Local evidence, not live status

The HTML snapshot is offline by design. Live availability is reported only after an explicit CLI check.
